## Summary

Peer-reviewed (EMNLP 2026) method for attributing faithfulness and citation errors in a multi-agent deep-research pipeline to the specific agent invocation that introduced them, with a four-type taxonomy (hallucination, uncited input reliance, uncited output, insufficient citations). Finds errors pass through the pipeline like a telephone game and that 84.7% of final-report errors in AI-Q originate at the orchestrator, ~31% of them hallucinations. Directly relevant to anyone designing a multi-worker research pipeline and needing per-stage accountability rather than end-of-pipeline evaluation.
