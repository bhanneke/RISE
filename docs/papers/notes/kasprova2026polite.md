## Summary

Moves sycophancy from the single-agent setting into collaborative multi-agent discussion, running controlled experiments with six open-source LLMs in which agents are given peer sycophancy rankings computed by static (pre-discussion) and dynamic (online) strategies. Supplying these sycophancy priors reduces the influence of sycophancy-prone peers, mitigates error cascades, and improves final discussion accuracy by an absolute 10.5%. A mechanism citation for why deliberative structures degrade, and evidence that the degradation is a property of the interaction design rather than of the underlying models.
