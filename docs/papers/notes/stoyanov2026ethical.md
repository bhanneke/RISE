## Summary

Develops a normative framework separating content origin, completion of human verification, responsibility assignment and accountable human ownership, arguing the ethical boundary of LLM-assisted research is set by adequate verification and accountable ownership rather than by how much of the work the machine did. Proposes an 'epistemic audit': a structured record of delegation, verification, provenance and responsibility that makes AI-assisted reasoning transparent and reviewable. Conceptual counterpart to the empirical work on where researchers actually draw the delegation line.
