## Summary

Position piece arguing that replacement of researchers is already underway, not as disappearance but as a shift from researcher-as-creator to researcher-as-curator: as agents generate hypotheses, papers and reviews, humans keep accountability while losing intellectual ownership. Frames the danger as humans ceasing to genuinely understand the science they sign. Useful conceptual vocabulary for the deskilling / cognitive-offloading strand of an IS argument.
