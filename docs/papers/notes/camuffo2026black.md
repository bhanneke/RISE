## Summary

Explicitly adopts a design view inspired by Herbert Simon's work on system architecture, arguing that purposeful AI system design - not access to generic models - is itself a dynamic capability, then builds Aristotle, an agentic multiagent system for theory-based strategic decision making, and tests a streamlined three-agent version against GPT-4o and a human-only condition with 976 managers. Experienced managers gain quality without confidence inflation while highly educated managers gain confidence without quality, establishing user-system-problem fit as a design dimension, and the paper abductively derives a five-dimensional taxonomy of the agentic-AI design space. This is the strongest management-venue precedent for treating multi-agent architecture as the object of theory rather than a black box, and the ISR paper needs it both to position its contribution and to borrow the design-space vocabulary.
