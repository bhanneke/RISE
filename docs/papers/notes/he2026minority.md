## Summary

States explicitly that multi-agent debate with majority voting rests on the Condorcet Jury Theorem's independent-errors assumption, and that shared pretraining corpora break it, causing the majority to systematically suppress correct minority opinions - a 'Minority Truth' effect present in roughly one in four divergent cases across six benchmarks, worth a 10-percentage-point theoretical recovery margin. Their Minority Sentinel meta-classifier recovers suppressed minorities at 81.2% flip precision with positive net gain on all six datasets. Evidence both that consensus aggregation destroys information and that traceable deliberation logs are themselves a usable epistemic artifact.
