## Summary

IS scholars (Fang, Ram, Xie, Chai) propose AI4CDS, a five-phase methodological framework for computational design science in which AI actively participates in problem formulation, resource construction, design search, evaluation and knowledge abstraction while the researcher retains domain grounding, admissibility, verification and scientific judgment. Collaboration is governed by graduated trust, reversibility, auditability and differentiated reproducibility, and the framework is instantiated in a full artifact (ChildRiskGuard, F1 0.769) with documented AI interactions, rejected alternatives and audit trails. This is the IS discipline writing its own methodological rules for agentic AI in design research, and reads as a direct entry to the ISR generative-AI methods special issue.
