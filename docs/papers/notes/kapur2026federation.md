## Summary

A controlled ablation on a production scientific-agent platform varying federation topology, harness type (classic RL versus LLM-driven), language model, and prompt expertise on a verifiable protein-characterization task, stratified by protein novelty. Model choice dominates prediction quality (Opus ~92-94% versus o4-mini ~40-50%) while federation topology imposes a negligible penalty, and a PPO policy reaches 88% at zero token cost with fastest latency and perfect consistency but yields no reasoning trace. Two things make it relevant: it is a rival finding that topology is second-order to model capability, a threat the ISR paper must confront head-on, and it renders the traceability-accuracy tradeoff concrete by showing the cheapest accurate configuration is the one with no auditable justification.
