## Summary

Experimental demonstration that decentralized populations of LLM agents spontaneously converge on universally adopted social conventions, and that strong collective biases emerge during this process even when agents exhibit no bias individually. It also shows committed minorities of adversarial LLM agents can impose alternative conventions on the larger population. The emergent-bias-from-unbiased-individuals result is the population-level analogue of the deliberation pathologies the paper's consensus condition should exhibit, and it carries the visibility an IS audience expects.
