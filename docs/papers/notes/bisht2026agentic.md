## Summary

Position paper naming four structural reasons agentic AI scientists work as co-scientists but not as autonomous discoverers: McNamara-fallacy problem selection, training corpora that omit tacit procedural and failure knowledge of lab practice, preference optimisation that compresses output diversity toward consensus, and benchmarks that measure single-turn prediction without physical-experiment feedback. Proposes simulations as training verifiers, persistent world models, and a centralised preregistration repository for AI-generated hypotheses — a governance idea directly transferable to IS.
