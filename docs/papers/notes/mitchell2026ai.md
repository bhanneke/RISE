## Summary

Position paper arguing that current agent design does not merely fail to support human oversight but actively degrades it, because the cognitive capacities oversight requires are themselves eroded by extended AI use. Connects the automation and HCI literatures to agent processes and specifies design-level affordances and organizational protocols meant to preserve critical judgement and counteract skill atrophy. The clearest recent statement of the deskilling-plus-oversight problem, and it transfers directly to the question of whether a researcher supervising an agent can still catch its errors.
