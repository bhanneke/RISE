## Summary

Elicits probability estimates from 15 LLMs on 254 binary prediction-market questions and compares classical against learned aggregators, finding a multilayer perceptron and a logistic regression outperform all individual models and all classical pooling rules. Direct evidence that the aggregation rule, not merely the existence of an ensemble, carries the performance gain - the central claim the paper's second factor tests. It also documents training-cutoff contamination as a pervasive confound, the frontier-versus-small-model gap collapsing from 35.8% to 8.9% on questions resolved after all cutoffs, a methodological warning for any research-forecasting task design.
