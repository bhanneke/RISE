## Summary

Argues that disagreement about whether generative AI enriches or impoverishes culture stems from conflating novelty, a property of single artifacts, with diversity, a property of populations, and that creativity is best treated as a property of hybrid collectives rather than individuals. AI-assisted ideation reliably raises individual novelty while narrowing aggregate diversity, but mixed groups can both outperform and out-diversify homogeneous ones because humans and models search in complementary ways. The decisive sentence for this paper is the authors' conclusion that what determines the outcome is composition - which agents are present, in what proportion, and how they are connected - which is the ISR thesis restated for creativity and gives the paper a principled reason to report novelty and diversity as two separate dependent variables.
