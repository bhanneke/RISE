## Summary

Identifies two distinct mechanisms suppressing diversity in LLM ideation: individual-level fixation, where early outputs constrain subsequent ideation, and collective-level failure of knowledge partitioning, because LLMs aggregate knowledge into one unified distribution rather than occupying distinct regions of the knowledge space as human populations do. Four studies show CoT prompting fixes fixation and ordinary (rather than celebrity-creative) personas fix partitioning, and combining both produces the highest diversity, outperforming humans. Supplies both a theoretical vocabulary and a measurement approach for the paper's exploration/novelty outcome, from a management and marketing framing rather than a pure NLP one.
