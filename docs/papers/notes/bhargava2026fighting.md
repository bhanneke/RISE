## Summary

Models deployment of LLMs as structured, trained frontline reviewers inside journal workflows, comparing traditional peer review against an AI-infused pipeline analytically and showing how governed AI reconfigures reviewer effort while improving turnaround time and decision accuracy. Insists implementation requires transparency, author contestability and human-in-the-loop oversight. The management-side counterpart to the Organization Science 'More versus better' peer-review editorial and the JAIS peer-review special issue already in the catalog; published just outside the window (2026-08-07) but centrally on-angle and missing.
