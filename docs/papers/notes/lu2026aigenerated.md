## Summary

Official NeurIPS 2026 report on running an AI-writing detector (Pangram) across 971 position-paper submissions: 28.2% flagged as substantially AI-written at default settings (12.7% under a stricter window analysis), against ~1% at FAccT 2025. 178 submissions (18.4%) desk-rejected without appeal and 123 conditionally rejected. The most concrete venue-level enforcement datapoint on machine-written submissions.
