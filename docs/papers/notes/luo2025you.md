## Summary

Controlled audit of the internal workflow of two prominent open-source AI-scientist systems, isolating four failure modes: inappropriate benchmark selection, data leakage, metric misuse and post-hoc selection bias. Finds real instances of each across a severity spectrum that are easy to miss from the finished paper alone, and shows that trace logs plus code make detection far more effective. Concludes with a concrete policy recommendation that venues evaluating AI-generated research mandate submission of traces and code — directly actionable for an IS argument about verifiable research pipelines.
