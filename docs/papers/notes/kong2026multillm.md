## Summary

The strongest existing negative result against the premise that multi-agent LLM systems expand exploration: across model families and simulations of 200-1,000 rounds, inference-only multi-LLM systems converge in semantic representation despite lexical variation, and thirteen interventions (decoding parameters, prompt design, agent composition, activation engineering, RL) all fail to restore diversity. Crucially the authors rule out alignment and conformity bias as the mechanism and attribute collapse to intrinsic properties of autoregressive generation. This is the paper the exploration/novelty construct must be defended against: if collapse is architecture-invariant, the claim that structure moves novelty needs an explicit scope condition, and the open-ended research-forecasting task (not a closed loop) is the natural boundary to argue.
