## Summary

Clean quasi-experimental economics of a frontier AI model in science: after AlphaFold2's July 2021 release the rate of experimental structure determination barely moves, but basic research on previously unstudied proteins rises 15-40% relative to proteins that already had structural data, i.e. AI redirects attention rather than raising raw output. No detectable spillover yet into early-stage drug development. The best-identified counterpoint to the 'AI raises productivity' narrative and a direct empirical companion to the Nature narrowing-of-focus result.
