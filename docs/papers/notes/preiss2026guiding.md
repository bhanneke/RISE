## Summary

Uses 98 Allied Health Professions outputs with specialist human review reports and adjudicated 1-4 reference scores to test whether supplying an oracle score changes not just an LLM reviewer's accuracy but the evidence its review cites. Oracle guidance improves scoring accuracy without the model simply copying the score, and corrected mismatches shift the review frame; alignment is direction-dependent, with LLMs covering human strength/upgrade points far more reliably than expert downgrade evidence. Useful because it evaluates LLM review at the level of evidence rather than score correlation.
