## Summary

Turns each scientific paper into a training-and-evaluation environment for research planning by synthesizing the question from goal and background while deriving grading criteria from method and experiments, cutting criterion leakage to 3.7% against 11.9-34.1% in existing rubric datasets. Releases PaperGym-20k plus the PaperGym-Innov and PaperGym-Design benchmarks covering methodological innovation and experimental design. Important for the leakage problem that quietly inflates most paper-derived research-agent benchmarks.
