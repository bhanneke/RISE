## Summary

Distinguishes semantic diversity of observed outputs from generative-process diversity - differences between the processes capable of producing those outputs - and measures the latter using Normalised Compression Distance between raw model outputs residualised against a permutation control, across 38 language models. The measure recovers population structure that semantic similarity misses and predicts chance-corrected correlated failure across ten disjoint benchmark families, with a cross-benchmark partial rank association of -0.216 (95% interval -0.309 to -0.122) that is negative on all ten. This supplies the ISR paper with a defensible operationalization of the diversity a polyarchy or committee actually requires, plus a warning that embedding-similarity novelty and diversity metrics measure the wrong construct.
