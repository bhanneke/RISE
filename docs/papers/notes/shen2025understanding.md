## Summary

Provides a causal framework for how agent outputs, correct or erroneous, propagate under communication topologies of varying density, and finds moderately sparse structures best balance error suppression against useful information sharing. The mechanism-level counterpart to the Sah-Stiglitz hierarchy/polyarchy trade-off (type I versus type II error filtering) expressed in LLM-agent terms. Their EIB-learner mixes dense and sparse patterns, speaking directly to why an intermediate structure may dominate pure hierarchy or pure polyarchy.
