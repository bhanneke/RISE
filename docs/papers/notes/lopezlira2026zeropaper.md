## Summary

Companion paper to the catalogued `zeropaper` pipeline, dated 1 May
2026. The system takes a research domain as input and produces a
publication-candidate manuscript with no human intervention between
launch and completion: ten numbered stages, six adversarial gates,
about thirty specialised agents, and finished LaTeX for economics at
roughly two dollars per paper amortised under a flat-fee Claude Code
Max subscription.
