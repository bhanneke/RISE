## Summary

A synthetic-control study of 2,080 monthly observations across twenty arXiv archives (Jan 2018 - Aug 2026) documenting a large 2026 output regime shift concentrated in Mathematics: 47,127 list entries, 33.5% above 2025 and 11.9% above the synthetic counterfactual, robust across donor refits, placebos and alternative pre-periods. An author panel shows the repeated-output tail thickening (share producing 5+ submissions rising 2.45% to 3.80%, 10+ from 0.21% to 0.49%). Directly relevant as quantitative evidence on AI's productivity effect in fields with verifiable tasks, and it names verification, selection and attention as the binding governance constraints.
