## Summary

The headline Science study on LLMs and research output: 2.1M preprints (arXiv, bioRxiv, SSRN), 28K peer review reports and 246M document accesses. Reports 23.7-89.3% higher publication rates for LLM-adopting scientists depending on field and author background, a decoupling of linguistic complexity from substance ('linguistically complex but substantively underwhelming'), and broader engagement with monographs and newer, underused sources. Must be cited together with the PNAS rebuttal below, which disputes the causal identification.
