## Summary

The flagship peer-reviewed evidence on what AI does to science as a system: 41.3 million natural-science papers, ~311k AI-augmented. Individually, AI-using scientists publish 3.02x more, are cited 4.84x more and lead projects 1.37 years earlier; collectively, the number of topics studied shrinks 4.63% and engagement between scientists falls 22%, producing 'lonely crowds' concentrated in data-rich areas. The single strongest citation for the homogenisation/narrowing-of-science argument and an essential counterweight to productivity-only framings.
