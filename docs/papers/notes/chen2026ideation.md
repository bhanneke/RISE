## Summary

A pairwise-battle evaluation of research ideas from 14 frontier LLMs and 5 research-agent architectures, with all systems given identical shared literature contexts drawn from papers the human judges already know. Over 6,000 double-blind comparisons from 105 active CS researchers yield an Elo leaderboard of proposal-stage expert preference that is stable across annotator composition and domain. Two findings matter for anyone assessing AI research output: agent scaffolding sometimes helps and sometimes underperforms its own backbone, and LLM judges still cannot reproduce expert preference (best judge 72.56% soft accuracy on overall quality).
