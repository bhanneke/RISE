## Summary

Measures identity-level citation hallucination (non-existent works, substantial author-list mismatches) in accepted camera-ready ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS and USENIX Security papers using RefChecker, an open-sourced multi-source verification pipeline. Reference-level rates stay under 1%, but roughly one in twenty 2025 NeurIPS and USENIX Security papers carries at least two likely hallucinated references, with post-ChatGPT increases and hallucinations even in award-winning papers. Shows peer review does not enforce citation integrity while auditing costs about $0.04 per paper — a clean argument for automated integrity infrastructure.
