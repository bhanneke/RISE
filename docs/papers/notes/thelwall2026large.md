## Summary

Large-N test for topic bias when LLMs score research quality: 73,489 articles from 15 health and life sciences journals, comparing title/abstract words in high- versus low-scoring articles for two models. GPT-OSS-120B favours viruses, genes and cells and penalises surveys, patients and students; Gemma 3 27B systematically diverges from it (notably scoring machine-learning research higher), which proves at least one of the two carries genuine AI bias rather than tracking quality. Scores also shift between full-text and title+abstract inputs. Direct evidence for the reliability question in AI-assisted research evaluation and reviewing.
