## Summary

Head-to-head quality benchmark of four AI-Scientist systems (Sakana AI Scientist v1 and v2, CycleResearcher, Data-to-Paper) on a common set of 15 research proposals, producing 60 machine-written papers scored by three frontier LLM reviewers on originality, rigour, clarity and significance. Human/benchmark papers score roughly 2x higher than every autonomous system, and inter-model agreement is high between Gemini and Claude but weak for GPT-5.4. Directly useful evidence for the 'AI-generated paper quality / slop' argument.
