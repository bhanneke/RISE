## Summary

Controlled cross-provider comparison of GPT-5.4, Gemini 3.1 Pro and Claude Opus 4.6 against human reviews and final decisions on 300 topic-matched ICLR 2026 submissions (oral / poster / rejected, decision info stripped). All three models separate accept from reject but none reproduces the oral-versus-poster gradient; scoring patterns are provider-specific, and LLMs emphasise missing baselines where humans raise computational efficiency. Shows that coarse decision alignment does not imply agreement with fine-grained human reviewing priorities.
