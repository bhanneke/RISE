## Summary

HKUDS's end-to-end system covering literature review, hypothesis
generation, algorithm implementation and manuscript preparation with
minimal human intervention, together with Scientist-Bench, a benchmark
of state-of-the-art papers across AI research domains with guided and
open-ended tasks. The authors report high implementation success and
papers approaching human-level quality. Code at
github.com/HKUDS/AI-Researcher (about 5,800 stars, no licence declared,
last pushed October 2025).
