## Summary

Uses a masked-citation task over 1,746 NLP conference papers and 63,000+ citation contexts to compare how LLMs versus human authors cite. LLMs cite significantly less critically, over-reference popular and established work (especially in contrasting citations), and reach socially distant authors rather than close collaborators. The double-edged framing - wider citation reach but reduced critical engagement and amplified visibility bias - is a concrete mechanism for how AI-assisted writing reshapes the scholarly record.
