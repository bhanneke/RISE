## Summary

Large-scale empirical evaluation of over 350 LLMs showing substantial correlation in model errors - on one leaderboard dataset models agree 60% of the time when both are wrong - driven by shared architectures and providers, and crucially still high for larger, more accurate models even with distinct architectures and providers. The empirical foundation for why the independence assumption behind Condorcet and Sah-Stiglitz polyarchy results fails for populations of LLM agents. They trace downstream consequences for LLM-as-judge evaluation and hiring, framed as algorithmic monoculture.
