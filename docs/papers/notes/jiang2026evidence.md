## Summary

Argues that existing dynamic-topology methods suffer 'structure-knowledge misalignment' because retrieval is treated as a reactive tool rather than a determinant of collaboration structure, producing redundant interactions or insufficient verification on knowledge-intensive tasks. K-GAT conditions autoregressive graph generation on external evidence and beats an LLM-Debate baseline by 15.7 points on GPQA at under half the token cost. Useful as a contrast case: it makes structure a function of the evidence base, which is a competing explanation the paper should acknowledge when attributing epistemic quality to structure crossed with aggregation rule on a research-forecasting task.
