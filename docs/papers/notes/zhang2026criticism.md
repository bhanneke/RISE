## Summary

Recasts AI-assisted reviewing as evidence-guided refinement of a structured concern set, treating omission (missing a consequential weakness) and overcritique (retaining an allegation the evidence does not support) as separate risks requiring opposite corrections - a distinction aggregate review-quality scores hide. EquiReview-R resolves concerns against localized evidence, searches for missing issues, and returns stop/continue/defer. On a frozen cohort of unseen papers it meets a prespecified non-inferiority criterion for major omission while cutting major overcritique from 15.5% to 8.1%, with computation-matched controls showing the gain comes from revision rather than extra inference. Ships ReviewTrace, an evidence-linked corpus for studying review revision and provenance.
