## Summary

Interview study with 21 doctoral students across disciplines on how they negotiate GenAI in scholarly work. Two findings travel well: AI functions as an access infrastructure that lowers linguistic or technical barriers depending on disciplinary demand, and doctoral labour is shifting from PRODUCTION toward VERIFICATION, with students bearing responsibility for judging the accuracy, legitimacy, ownership and defensibility of machine-assisted work while institutional policy stays ambiguous. The 'production to verification' framing is directly usable for the deskilling / division-of-labour argument.
