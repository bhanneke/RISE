## Summary

Csaszar's own current statement of how aggregation structures reshape collective preferences, and therefore the natural bridge from the paper's declared theory base to its design: unanimity approximates the pointwise minimum of member utilities and amplifies risk aversion, while polyarchy approximates the pointwise maximum and promotes risk-seeking. It supplies a formal mechanism for why the aggregation rule should move exploration and novelty rather than just accuracy, which is exactly the outcome dimension this paper claims benchmark studies miss. Citing the theory base's living author on aggregation structure is also a positioning asset for an ISR submission.
