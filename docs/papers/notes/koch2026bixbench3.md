## Summary

FutureHouse-affiliated benchmark measuring whether agents can take raw biological data through to scientific results at the scale of a complete research study: 20 tasks, 138 artifacts, frontier models scoring 0.00-0.48. Performance degrades with raw dataset size (0.36 under 100 GB vs 0.10 over 100 GB) and with the number of sequential analysis steps, and an average task consumed 6.8 hours, 102M tokens and $43. Rare in reporting the compute-and-cost envelope alongside capability, which is what makes agentic research pipelines comparable.
