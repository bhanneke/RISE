## Summary

Evidence-based audit of arXiv's own policy decision to ban unpublished CS review papers on the grounds that they are largely LLM-generated. Using two detection methods it confirms LLM content is more prevalent in review papers, but shows that in absolute counts LLM-generated NON-review papers are almost six times more numerous, so the policy misses most of the problem while hitting some subfields hard (Computers & Society faces potential cuts of ~50%). A model for evaluating publishing-platform policy with data rather than assertion.
