## Summary

Benchmark aimed specifically at ACADEMIC deep-research agents rather than general web search: 200 human-annotated instances across 10 academic domains, covering both research and review papers, with a modular evaluation paradigm that separates planning, retrieval and reasoning and runs both end-to-end (agents) and isolated (backbone LLM) modes. Finds agents strong in narrow specialisms but weak at multi-source retrieval and cross-field consistency, and identifies high-level planning as the binding constraint. Complements the AstaBench/AIRS-Bench family already in the KB by targeting the literature-synthesis stage.
