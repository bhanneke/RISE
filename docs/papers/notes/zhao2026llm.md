## Summary

Audits 111 million references across 2.5 million papers on arXiv, bioRxiv, SSRN and PubMed Central and estimates ~146,932 hallucinated citations in 2025 alone, concentrated in papers with AI-writing indicators and among early-career researchers. Adds an inequality finding that false attributions disproportionately accrue to prominent and male scholars, amplifying existing recognition disparities, and argues current moderation is inadequate. Written by a strong social-science/scientometrics team (Stuart, De Vaan, Ginsparg, Yin) so it travels well into management venues.
