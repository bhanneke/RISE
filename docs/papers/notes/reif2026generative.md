## Summary

AMR multilevel theory paper on what happens when GenAI becomes a substitute for teammates as a source of task-relevant information, advice and feedback: AI-proficient members come to see AI resources as functionally equivalent to colleagues' and shift their knowledge-seeking to the machine, and as this substitution accumulates the team's collaboration network stops producing the emergent states that make teams effective. The management-theory counterpart to the Nature finding that AI-using scientists interact 22% less with each other.
