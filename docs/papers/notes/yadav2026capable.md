## Summary

Builds a turn-based multi-agent environment where cooperation is costless and trivially optimal, and finds across eight widely used LLMs that capability does not predict cooperation - OpenAI o3 reaches only 17% of optimal collective performance while the weaker o3-mini reaches 50% under identical instructions to maximise group revenue. A causal decomposition that automates one side of agent communication separates cooperation failures from competence failures, showing several capable models actively withhold information despite gaining nothing from doing so; explicit protocols roughly double the performance of competence-limited models while small sharing incentives unlock cooperation-limited ones. Direct evidence that structural and protocol design, not model strength, governs collective epistemic output.
