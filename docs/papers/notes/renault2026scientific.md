## Summary

Econometric demolition of the identification behind the Science productivity result: defining treatment as the first paper DETECTED as LLM-assisted mechanically produces positive event-study dynamics even with zero causal effect, because high-output months are more likely to yield a detected paper. Reconstructed arXiv data show random assignments, neutral-keyword triggers, inverted treatment and pre-ChatGPT placebos all reproduce the same pattern, as do zero-effect simulations. Essential for any IS/econ author who wants to cite AI-productivity evidence responsibly.
