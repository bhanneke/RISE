## Summary

The largest public deployment of a fully automated AI-for-AI research system to date: FARS autonomously ran ideation, planning, experimentation and writing through stage-specific agents on a shared workspace and produced 166 complete papers across 67 fine-grained AI/ML topics, deliberately preserving intermediate artefacts as an auditable corpus rather than a curated highlight reel. Evaluated with 282 structured volunteer reviews over 140 papers including integrity checks and LLM-use disclosure; some outputs are review-worthy, but narrow experimental scope, methodological limits and integrity issues recur. Note: FARS is the reference system in the 'Can AI Evaluate AI Scientists?' benchmark.
