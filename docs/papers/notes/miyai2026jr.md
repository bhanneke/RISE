## Summary

Peer-reviewed (TMLR 2026) autonomous AI-scientist system that mimics a novice student's workflow — take a mentor-supplied baseline paper, diagnose its limitations, form improvement hypotheses, iterate experiments, write the paper — applied to real NeurIPS, IJCV and ICLR works. Notable because it pairs the system with an explicit risk report and with submissions to Agents4Science, and it out-scores existing fully automated systems under AI reviewers while documenting where human expertise remains necessary.
