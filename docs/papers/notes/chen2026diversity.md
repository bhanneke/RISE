## Summary

Directly attributes diversity loss in open-ended ideation to the interaction structure rather than model insufficiency, which is this paper's core claim stated in the negative. It reports three findings the paper can build on: authority-driven (hierarchical) dynamics suppress semantic diversity relative to junior-dominated groups, dense communication topologies accelerate premature convergence, and group-size scaling shows diminishing returns; it also names a compute-efficiency paradox in which stronger aligned models yield less marginal diversity. This is the closest empirical precedent for structure-moves-novelty and must be cited and distinguished, since it measures idea diversity but not justification soundness or traceability.
