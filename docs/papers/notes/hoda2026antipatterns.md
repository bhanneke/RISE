## Summary

Senior qualitative methodologists catalogue antipatterns in AI-assisted qualitative data analysis, grouped by escalating impact into Dangerous Drivers, Operational Missteps and Analytical Failures, explicitly so that reviewers gain vocabulary for calling out failed practice and the field avoids accelerated production of low-quality research. Aimed at software engineering but the failure modes transfer directly to qualitative and interpretive IS work. Concrete methodological guidance rather than general exhortation.
