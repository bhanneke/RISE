## Summary

Addresses the underspecification problem in open-ended research tasks: AutoSciRub induces a task-specific executable rubric *before* execution, decomposing the instruction into atomic scientific goals grounded in literature and task-visible data, then uses criterion-level verification to drive revision of the report and its artifacts. Gains of about 2-3 points on ResearchClawBench across three backbones and three harnesses, and 16.8 points on a 20-task AstaBench E2E Discovery subset. Useful as the constructive counterpart to the audit/benchmark papers, since it makes evidential requirements explicit rather than post hoc.
