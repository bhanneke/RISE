## Summary

Surveys 35 AI-scientist systems with full-text coding of 26 entries along seven audit dimensions including novelty verification and result-selection disclosure, and reports that code release is now common (83% of 24 runnable systems) while reproducibility-grade and claim-verification artifacts are not (38% release seeds or execution traces; 38% report any novelty-verification method). Its central claim is that the field's bottleneck is no longer whether agents can complete research tasks but whether reviewers can verify the claims those agents produce. The field-level warrant for making transparency and traceability first-class outcome variables rather than accuracy alone, and the most current (Aug 2026) survey of research-agent systems.
