## Summary

NeurIPS 2026 runs an actual randomised experiment inside its own review process: reviewer-paper pairs are assigned by constrained optimisation to unassisted review, open-ended LLM assistance inside OpenReview, or structured task-specific assistance, with blinded area chairs rating review quality and all LLM conversations logged and visible to the experiment chairs. IRB-exempt under UT Austin protocol STUDY00009083. The clearest example of a venue treating AI-in-review as an empirical question rather than a policy edict, and a template for studying AI in the IS review process.
