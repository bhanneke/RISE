## Summary

End-to-end benchmark that asks six autonomous research systems (AI Scientist v1/v2, Agent Laboratory, CycleResearcher, AI Researcher, Tiny Scientist) to reproduce ICML 2025 outstanding papers, yielding 45 manuscripts judged by both automated conference-style review and structured expert human review. Automated review accepted 10 of 37 valid submissions, but human reviewers found methodological flaws, hallucinated results and reproducibility failures in all systems, and 59% of the automatically accepted papers contained fabricated or unsupported claims. Also shows compute does not buy quality: the cheapest system beat the most expensive despite a 38x token gap.
