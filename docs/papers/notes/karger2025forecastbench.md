## Summary

The standard dynamic benchmark for AI forecasting: 1,000 automatically generated and regularly updated questions about future events, with a 200-question subset used to compare expert forecasters, the general public, and LLMs on the same items. Expert forecasters outperform the top-performing LLM at p < 0.001. Because the questions are about not-yet-resolved events, it is the canonical solution to the contamination problem that Douven flags, and it is the natural reference point for calibrating how hard the paper's research-forecasting task is and how its agent populations compare to human benchmarks.
