## Summary

OLDER ITEM (Feb 2026) included because the knowledge base has nothing on journal AI-disclosure policy effectiveness. Examines 5,114 journals and over 5.2 million papers: roughly 70% of journals have adopted AI policies, mostly disclosure requirements, yet detected AI-assisted writing rose sharply across disciplines with no significant difference between journals that have policies and those that do not. Full-text analysis of 164,000 publications finds only about 76 of the 75,000 post-2023 papers explicitly disclose AI use - roughly 0.1% - which makes declarative policy the wrong instrument and argues for verifiable, accountable frameworks instead.
