## Summary

Treats cost and accuracy in multi-role LLM teams (planner, executor, verifier) as properties of the assignment of models to roles rather than of any single model, and introduces AgentCARD, a benchmark combining role-decomposed evaluation, unified cost modelling and Pareto-frontier analysis. Heterogeneous teams achieve up to 44% accuracy improvements or up to 12x cost reduction, and role bottlenecks vary by domain. The cleanest available treatment of the cost side of organizational design, which the paper needs if it claims a structure is better on epistemic quality but more expensive.
