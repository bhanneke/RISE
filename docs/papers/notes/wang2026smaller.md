## Summary

Large-scale observational study of 147,074 full-text PLoS and Nature-portfolio papers since 2020 showing that teams using AI-assisted writing are systematically younger and smaller, reversing the long 'Big Science' trend toward ever-larger teams, without any loss of impact — in fact a higher probability of producing highly cited work. Uses OLS, quantile, Poisson and logistic regression plus propensity-score matching. A direct empirical handle on how AI restructures the division of labour in research teams.
