## Summary

Addresses the measurement problem this paper faces on its exploration outcome: how to distinguish and quantify exploration and exploitation from an agent's observed actions without access to its internal policy. They build controllable environments (partially observable 2D grid maps with an unknown task DAG) whose generation can be tuned to emphasise exploration or exploitation difficulty, and design a policy-agnostic metric for exploration and exploitation errors. Frontier models struggle, with distinct failure modes by model, and both dimensions improve substantially with minimal harness engineering. Directly citable for operationalising March's exploration/exploitation distinction in LLM-agent terms rather than by analogy.
