## Summary

ICML 2026's dual-policy mechanism is a genuine institutional innovation: authors declare whether their submission requires Policy A (LLM reviewing strictly prohibited) or permits Policy B (LLMs may be used to understand the paper and polish prose, but never to produce strengths/weaknesses, key points, outlines or the review itself), reviewers declare which policy they follow, and the system matches them. Reviewers remain fully responsible for review content under both. A concrete, citable design for consent-based governance of AI in peer review.
