## Summary

Builds the first peer-review-specific hallucination benchmark: aligned triples of paper content, human-written reviews and hallucination-injected reviews, annotated for detection, classification and localization, with an induced review-specific hallucination taxonomy. Experiments over 12K papers and 38K reviews show existing hallucination detectors cannot separate fabricated claims from legitimate harsh critique. Crucially, the HalluPeer patterns are shown to occur in authentic reviews too, which makes this directly usable evidence about the reliability of LLM review assistants.
