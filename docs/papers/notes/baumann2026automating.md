## Summary

Peer-reviewed ICML 2026 oral position paper arguing that current AI systems should not produce paper reviews, grounded in an empirical human-versus-AI comparison on ICLR 2026 reviews. Documents two failure modes: a 'hivemind effect' of excessive agreement within and across papers that collapses perspective diversity, and 'paper laundering', where prompting an LLM to rewrite a paper materially raises AI review scores through style rather than science. Argues for a science of peer-review automation before deployment.
