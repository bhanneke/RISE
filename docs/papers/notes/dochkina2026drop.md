## Summary

The largest structure-varying experiment currently available: 25,000 tasks across 8 models, 4-256 agents and 8 coordination protocols spanning externally imposed hierarchy through emergent self-organization, with a 44% quality spread between protocols (Cohen's d=1.86). Two results bear directly on the paper's design: a minimally scaffolded hybrid protocol beats centralized coordination by 14%, and the benefit of imposed structure is conditional on model capability - strong models self-organize while models below a capability threshold still need rigid structure. That interaction is the main threat to external validity for any fixed-structure claim and should be addressed explicitly, but note the study is single-authored and reports a composite quality score rather than separable epistemic dimensions.
