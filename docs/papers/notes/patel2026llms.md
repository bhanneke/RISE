## Summary

Provides the base-rate evidence for the monoculture premise on the exact output dimension the paper cares about. Across three years of model releases, measured on Infinity-Chat100 (real-world open-ended user queries) and the Alternate Uses Task (an established psychometric creativity assessment), sentence-embedding similarity shows a statistically significant decrease in output diversity over time, i.e. models are converging in creative substance. If cross-model diversity is shrinking, then any exploration or novelty gain the paper attributes to architecture has to be shown to survive on a common backbone, which strengthens the case for structure as the lever. Explicitly preliminary, so cite for the trend rather than the magnitude.
