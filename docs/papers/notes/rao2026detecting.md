## Summary

Systematic measurement of citation-URL reliability across 10 models and agents on DRBench (53,090 URLs) and 3 models on ExpertQA (168,021 URLs, 32 academic fields): 3-13% of citation URLs are hallucinated with no Wayback Machine record, 5-18% do not resolve at all, and deep-research agents both cite more and hallucinate at higher rates than search-augmented LLMs. Non-resolving rates vary by field from 5.4% (Business) to 11.4% (Theology). Ships urlhealth, an open-source liveness checker that measurably reduces bad citations in agentic self-correction.
