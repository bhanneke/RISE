## Summary

Survey of 868 scientists who program, on GenAI adoption, tool preference and perceived productivity. Adoption is highest among students and less experienced programmers; scientists overwhelmingly prefer general conversational interfaces (ChatGPT) over developer-specific tools; and the strongest predictor of perceived productivity is the number of lines of generated code accepted at once — implying researchers gauge productivity by generation volume rather than validation. A sharp, quantitative deskilling/over-reliance datapoint for the research-pipeline argument.
