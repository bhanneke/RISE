## Summary

Rejects human-likeness as the evaluation target for LLM reviewing and instead annotates ICLR 2025 human and LLM review points against five theory-guided frameworks (Anderson, Toulmin, Graesser, SOLO, Hattie) to characterise what critique functions each performs. Humans supply more scientific framing, higher-order weaknesses and revision guidance; LLMs supply more explanatory depth and explicit argument structuring, and expert prompting amplifies rather than corrects those LLM tendencies. A methodologically careful alternative to overlap-based LLM-reviewer benchmarks.
