## Summary

Peer-reviewed (KDD 2026) benchmark that moves beyond code-and-data reproduction to genuine replication in the social and behavioural sciences, with human-verified replicable AND non-replicable claims so agents can be tested on identifying failures. Evaluates three stages — retrieving replication data, designing and executing experiments, interpreting results — and finds that agents design and run experiments competently but fail at sourcing the new data a replication requires. Built with the Center for Open Science; the most directly transferable replication benchmark for behavioural IS research.
