## Summary

Applies a detector trained on pre-LLM reviews to later review cycles at ICLR and Nature Communications, giving a clean temporal prevalence estimate: near-zero before 2022, then roughly 20% of ICLR reviews and 12% of Nature Communications reviews classified as AI-generated in 2025, with the sharpest NC jump between Q3 and Q4 2024. Extends the ICLR/EMNLP sentence-level estimates already in the KB (Liang et al.) into a journal setting and up to 2025.
