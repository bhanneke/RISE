## Summary

Benchmark of 40 end-to-end research tasks across 10 scientific domains, each grounded in a real published paper whose text is hidden at evaluation time, with expert-curated multimodal rubrics that score target-paper-level re-discovery. Seven auto-research agents and seventeen LLMs are evaluated: the best agent (Claude Code) averages 21.5 and the LLM frontier mean is 26.5, with failures concentrated in experimental-protocol mismatch, evidence mismatch and missing scientific core. Distinct from the AutoResearchClaw system paper (2605.20025) despite the similar name: this is an evaluation harness, not a system.
