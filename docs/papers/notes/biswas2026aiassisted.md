## Summary

The largest deployed field study of LLM reviewing to date: AI-generated reviews for all 22,977 AAAI-26 submissions, with survey evidence that authors and reviewers found the AI reviews useful and preferred them to human reviews on technical accuracy and research suggestions. Reports the safeguard architecture and shows the system beats naive LLM baselines at surfacing scientific weaknesses. The reference point for any IS argument about institutionalising AI in peer review.
