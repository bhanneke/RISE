## Summary

Demonstrates that AI-mediated peer review can be gamed by superficially rephrasing an abstract, with no change in scientific content and no knowledge of the reviewing model: ~38% attack success rate, +1.31 rating for Gemini 3 Flash and +0.88 for GPT-5.4 Mini on a 10-point scale, rising above 50% when the original AI review said reject. Costs about five minutes and one dollar per submission and is indistinguishable from ordinary copy-editing. The strongest current evidence that AI reviewers are not neutral evaluators.
