This page traces the streams of thought that converge into RISE.

## Planned sections

- **e-Science and research infrastructure (2000s).** Grid computing,
  cyberinfrastructure, data-intensive science.
- **Open and reproducible science (2010s).** Pre-registration, open
  data, replication crises, computational reproducibility.
- **Information Systems as discipline.** Design-science research;
  sociotechnical axis (Sarker et al., 2019); IS methodology in the
  GenAI era (Gopal et al., 2025; Abbasi et al., 2026 ISR call).
- **AI4Science and the agentic turn.** From narrow predictive models
  to tool-using agents (Toolformer; Schick et al., 2023) to autonomous
  "AI scientists" (Sakana, zeropaper, aixiv).
- **Critique and friction.** Hallucination, faithfulness, peer-review
  crises, anthropomorphism (Naddaf, 2025; Peter et al., 2025).
