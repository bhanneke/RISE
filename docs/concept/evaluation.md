# Evaluating RISE systems

> *Stub — content to be developed in Phase 2.*

How do we tell a good RISE system from a bad one? This is an open
methodological question.

This page will discuss the **evaluation of RISE systems as scholarly
artifacts** — distinct from, but related to, the [project-comparison
rubric](../../projects/EVALUATION.md) used in this knowledge base.

Planned sections:

- **What is being evaluated.** The pipeline, the artifacts it produces,
  the *research it enables*, or all three?
- **Output-level evaluation.** Faithfulness, factual accuracy,
  novelty, methodological soundness, peer-review readiness.
- **Process-level evaluation.** Reproducibility of the pipeline itself,
  determinism, auditability of intermediate artifacts.
- **Field-level evaluation.** Does deployment of RISE systems shift the
  composition of published research? (Cf. Toner-Rodgers, 2025;
  Gartenberg et al., 2026 on peer-review side effects.)
- **Benchmarks vs. case studies.** Why current benchmarks under-capture
  what matters; the role of replication tasks as evaluations.
